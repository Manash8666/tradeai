# Information Memorandum: Business Model & Revenue Valuation

## Abstract
The AI Trading Platform targets the high-net-worth retail and proprietary trading segments in India (NSE/BSE), providing institutional-grade algorithmic execution, Deep Learning anomaly detection (XAI), and zero-latency Options mapping.

## Revenue Generation Strategy (B2C & B2B)

**1. Retail Subscription Tier (B2C)**
- **Target:** Pro-Retail F&O Traders.
- **Offering:** Access to the structural Options Heat Field, Real-Time AI Z-Score signals, and Pre-Trade Risk guards.
- **Pricing:** ₹2,999 - ₹4,999 / month flat SaaS fee.

**2. Institutional License/Brokerage Share (B2B)**
- **Target:** Sub-brokers and prop-desks seeking whitelabel strategies.
- **Offering:** Raw `.ots` verified signal feeds, ClickHouse Audit trails natively integrated into their OMS.
- **Pricing:** 10-15% Profit Share / Brokerage rebate sharing models, or a flat ₹5 Lakh/annum organizational license.

## Valuation & Ask (M&A)

- **Target Acquisition Ask:** ₹25,000,000 - ₹50,000,000 (INR)
- **Valuation Logic:**
  - **ARR Multiplier:** Evaluated at standard 8x-10x Fintech SaaS ARR multipliers on projected Year 1 penetration (1,000 active sub users = ₹3.6 Cr ARR).
  - **Defensible IP Premium:** The valuation heavily weighs the integration of SEBI-compliant Hash-Chained Clickhouse ledgers and Bitcoin L2 OpenTimestamps anchors guaranteeing exact regulatory safe-harbors. 
  - **Asset Readiness:** Fully built containerized microservices ready for immediate Brokerage-API key insertion (Angel One/Zerodha).
