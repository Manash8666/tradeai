# Legal Addendum: Conditional Earnout Clause

This document outlines the drafted structural terminology intended for inclusion in the final **Asset Purchase Agreement (APA)** separating the initial acquisition capital from the conditional performance-based payout structures.

---

### Section 4. Earnout Provisions & Cryptographic Verification

**4.1 Trigger Metrics:**
The Seller shall be entitled to conditional post-closing compensation (the "Earnout Tranche") provided the Acquired Platform generates Annual Gross Revenue equal to or exceeding ₹1,000,000 (INR 1 Crore) within sixty (60) months following the Effective Closing Date.

**4.2 Payment Dispersal:**
Upon meeting the constraints defined in Section 4.1, the Buyer agrees to disperse a one-time cash equivalent of ₹15,000,000 (INR 1.5 Crores) via direct SEBI/RBI compliant bank transfer within thirty (30) business days.

**4.3 Cryptographic Verification (The Ledger):**
To prevent disputes regarding the absolute revenue metrics generated by the platform post-acquisition, the Buyer is mandatorily required to execute the cryptographic validation tool (`finance/revenue_hasher.py`) securely hashing the monthly localized Stripe/Bank CSV execution streams.
- The resulting SHA-256 Merkle Roots shall be anchored to a neutral Escrow Ledger (or public chain, such as OpenTimestamps).
- Any discrepancy between the reported fiat distributions and the Cryptographic Hash History shall automatically trigger an independent forensic audit funded by the Buyer.

**4.4 Independent Audit Rights:**
The Seller retains the right to request exactly one (1) external financial audit annually mapping the Buyer's internal gross accounting sequences natively against the absolute SHA-256 Hashes bound via Section 4.3.
